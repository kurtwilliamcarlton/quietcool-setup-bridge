HOME ASSISTANT YELLOW · GUIDED DEVICE SETUP

QuietCool Setup Bridge

A guided way to set up your QuietCool ESP32 without switching your phone off your home network.

Step 1 of 6
STEP 1 · WELCOME

Let's set up your QuietCool controller

This assistant connects your Home Assistant Yellow to the ESP32's temporary Wi-Fi, sends your home Wi-Fi information, checks the new connection, and helps clean up the temporary connection.

1Confirm Ethernet keeps Home Assistant safely online.
2Find and connect to QuietCool Fallback.
3Identify your ESP32 and configure its home Wi-Fi.
4Verify the ESP32 on your LAN and disable temporary Wi-Fi.

Nothing connects automatically. You will approve each change. The bridge never uploads firmware or operates the fan.

STEP 2 · SAFETY CHECK

Keep Home Assistant connected over Ethernet

Before using the Wi-Fi adapter, your Yellow must have its Ethernet cable plugged in and an active Ethernet IP address. Wi-Fi setup is locked if this cannot be confirmed.

Checking Ethernet…

If the check fails, verify the Ethernet cable and open Home Assistant Settings → System → Network.

STEP 3 · CONNECT DEVICE

Find the ESP32's setup Wi-Fi

Put the ESP32 into fallback/setup mode so it broadcasts QuietCool Fallback. Your Yellow will only use its secondary Wi-Fi adapter (wlan0); Ethernet stays primary.

Checking Yellow Wi-Fi…

Scanning does not connect automatically.

If already connected to QuietCool Fallback, you can continue without reconnecting.

STEP 4 · IDENTIFY DEVICE

Make sure this is your QuietCool ESP32

The bridge contacts the captive portal at 192.168.4.1 and checks the device name and MAC before allowing credentials to be sent.

STEP 5 · HOME WI-FI

Give the ESP32 your home Wi-Fi

Select a nearby home network or type its name. This is the network the ESP32 will use after setup.

The bridge sends the password only when you approve. The device's ESPHome captive-portal connection uses local unencrypted HTTP.

Once submitted, the ESP32 might stop broadcasting its fallback network. This is normal.

STEP 6 · VERIFY & FINISH

Confirm the ESP32 joined your home network

Find the ESP32's new LAN IP in your router, or check quietcool.local from a browser on your home network. Then enter its IP here to test whether the Yellow can reach its web server over Ethernet.

Not verified yet. This is a network check, not confirmation that Home Assistant has added the ESPHome integration.

Disconnect temporary setup Wi-Fi

After LAN verification, disconnect and disable the Yellow's wlan0 so it won't rejoin an ESP32 fallback network after a future factory reset.

Setup finished!

The ESP32 answered on your home network, and temporary Yellow Wi-Fi is disabled.

Next outside this app: In Home Assistant, go to Settings → Devices & services → Add integration → ESPHome. Add the controller by its new LAN IP if Home Assistant did not discover it automatically. Then verify the fan entities work.

If verification fails, do not assume setup succeeded. You can return to the previous step, confirm the password, and check the router. If you need to leave now, you can still manually disable wlan0 in Home Assistant's Network settings.
