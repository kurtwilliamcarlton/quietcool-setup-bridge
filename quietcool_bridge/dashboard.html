Native Wi-Fi provisioning through ESPHome

Configure home Wi-Fi

Select a network seen by the ESP32 or enter a network name manually. Your password is sent through Home Assistant to the ESP32. The bridge does not store it. The ESP32 connection uses unencrypted HTTP.

What happens after saving?

1ESP32 saves the new Wi-Fi settings.
2It attempts to join your home network; QuietCool Fallback may disappear.
3Check your router / Home Assistant ESPHome integration or try quietcool.local on your home network.

If setup fails, confirm the Yellow's Wi-Fi interface still shows an address on 192.168.4.x and the ESP32 is broadcasting its fallback AP.
